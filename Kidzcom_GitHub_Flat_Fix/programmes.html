Around the school day

Care that fits around school.

Before and after school should feel different from school itself. Children need clear routines and good supervision, but they also need choices, movement, friends and time to breathe.

Parent information & enrolment

Before school

A steady start to the day.

Children have time to arrive, settle in, see their friends and get ready for the day ahead.

What to expect

A calm range of activities, a ride to school and, if they play their cards right, maybe a hot chocolate.

Hours, price & drop-offs

Opening: 7:00am
Fee: $6.50 per hour

We drop children off at St Joseph’s School Stratford, Stratford Primary School, Ngaere School, Midhirst School and Avon School.

After school

Space to unwind, play and get involved.

Once children arrive at Kidz.com, they can choose how they want to move into the afternoon.

They might make something, join a sport or group challenge, continue a longer project, play outside or spend time with friends. Staff are there to supervise, join in and notice when somebody needs help finding their place.

  • School collection
  • Afternoon tea provided
  • Creative activities
  • Indoor & outdoor play
  • Group challenges
  • Time with friends
  • Care until 5:30pm

Hours, price & school collection

Fee: $6.50 per hour
Final collection: 5:30pm

Afternoon tea is provided each day.

We collect from St Joseph’s School Stratford, Stratford Primary School, Ngaere School and Midhirst School.

Space to get outside

More than 1,000 m² of outdoor space.

Kids love being outdoors to unwind after their school day.

Our large grounds include a big field and plenty of hard-surface space, giving children room for active games or time to do their own thing.

  • Climbing frame
  • Basketball hoop
  • Scooters
  • Field games

How school collection works

We collect them from school.

A Kidz.com van and staff member arrive at each school with the day’s collection list. We check each child off before leaving and bring them back to Kidz.com—no extra organising at the school gate for you.

If your child will be away

If your child is booked and won’t be coming, please tell us before school finishes because our team will otherwise be expecting to collect them.
