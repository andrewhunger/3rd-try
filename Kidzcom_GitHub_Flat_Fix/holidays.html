Term 3 programme

Two weeks of big holiday fun.

Pirates, MasterChef, bowling and mini putt, mad science, sports, carnival games, scooters, Megabounce, squishy activities and PJ Day.

Standard days are $45. The current Wednesday trip days cost an additional $20. Other activities and games are available each day, so children still have choices beyond the featured theme.

How the day works

Planned activities and genuine free choice.

Children might join the day’s main challenge, choose another activity, continue a project from earlier in the week or simply spend time playing with friends.

  • Craft & making
  • Sport & active play
  • Baking
  • Competitions
  • Longer projects
  • Trips out
  • Free play

Food during the day

We provide morning tea and afternoon tea. Please send your child with a packed lunch and a named drink bottle.

Trips and special days

Big highlights to look forward to.

School holidays need big highlights to look forward to, and we love taking the kids on memorable trips.

Trip days cost extra, but we think they are totally worth it. Potential destinations include:

  • Megabounce
  • Bowlarama
  • The Running Grid
  • Kidz Korner
  • Tawhiti Museum

Help with holiday costs

OSCAR subsidy may cover up to 50 hours a week.

Eligible families can receive help with approved school-holiday care. Apply before the programme begins whenever possible.
