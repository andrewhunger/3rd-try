How to enrol

Getting started is simple.

  1. Get in touch
    Call, email or use the enquiry form.
  2. Tell us what care you need
    Let us know your child’s school, the type of care you need and the days you are looking for.
  3. Come in for a tour if you would like
    Meet us and have a look around Kidz.com.
  4. Complete the paperwork
    We can help you through it.
