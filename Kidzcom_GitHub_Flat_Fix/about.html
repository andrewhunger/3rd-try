About Kidz.com

Children don’t need another classroom after school.

They need choices, space to move, time with friends, helpful adults and something interesting to get involved in.

Who we are

Part of the Stratford community.

Kidz.com is an OSCAR programme run by MountainView Vineyard Church. We provide before-school, after-school and holiday care for local families.

Our programme is designed around safety, fun, connection and growth. Those words only matter if they shape what staff actually do: supervise well, plan things worth joining, know the children and communicate with parents.

Meet the team

The people who make Kidz.com happen.

They plan activities, drive vans, join games, solve practical problems and notice when somebody needs a hand. Tap or hover on a photograph to reveal its alter-ego version.

All names, roles, staff photographs, alter egos and biographies will be confirmed before launch. The image timer is staggered so the whole page does not move at once.

Working with families

Parents and whānau know their children best.

We want communication to be easy, whether you are telling us something important, asking a quick question, raising a concern or hearing about something great your child did that afternoon.
