About Kidz.com

Children don’t need another classroom after school.

They need choices, space to move, time with friends, helpful adults and something interesting to get involved in.

Who we are

Part of the Stratford community.

Kidz.com is an OSCAR programme run by MountainView Vineyard Church. We provide before-school, after-school and holiday care for local families.

Our programme is designed around safety, fun, connection and growth. Those words only matter if they shape what staff actually do: supervise well, plan things worth joining, know the children and communicate with parents.
