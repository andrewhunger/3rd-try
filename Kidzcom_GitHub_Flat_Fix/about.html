About Kidz.com

Children don’t need another classroom after school.

They need choices, space to move, time with friends, helpful adults and something interesting to get involved in.

25 years in Stratford

Built to help local families.

Kidz.com began 25 years ago because helping Stratford families is part of the mission of MountainView Vineyard Church.

What began as a practical response to a local need has grown into before-school, after-school and holiday care for generations of Stratford children.

Our approach

Care should feel different from school.

Children have already spent a full day following timetables, concentrating and meeting expectations. At Kidz.com, there is room to breathe.

We provide clear routines and thoughtful supervision, but children also get choice: run outside, make something, join a game, spend time with friends or slow down for a while.

What matters to us

Safe · Fun · Connect · Grow

Four simple ideas shape how we plan, supervise and build relationships with children and families.

01 · SAFE

Safety comes first.

Staff supervise actively, notice what is happening and respond early. Clear routines, sound policies and good communication help children feel secure enough to relax and enjoy themselves.

02 · FUN

Fun with some thought behind it.

We plan activities worth joining—not just things to fill time. There is also plenty of room for children’s own ideas, active play, imagination and the occasional unnecessarily serious competition.

03 · CONNECT

Children should be known.

We learn names, interests and friendships, and we pay attention when something does not seem quite right. Belonging begins when children know the adults around them genuinely care.

04 · GROW

Room to try something new.

Children can build confidence, practise getting along with others, discover new interests and take on challenges at their own pace—with encouragement close by when they need it.

MountainView Vineyard

A practical way to serve Stratford.

Kidz.com is run by MountainView Vineyard Church and grew from the church’s commitment to helping local families.

It is a community service open to families across Stratford. The relationship gives Kidz.com its history and heart; the day-to-day focus remains straightforward—reliable care, good people and a place where children can belong.
