About Kidz.com

Children don’t need another classroom after school.

They need choices, space to move, time with friends, helpful adults and something interesting to get involved in.

Who we are

Part of the Stratford community.

Kidz.com began 25 years ago because helping Stratford families is part of the mission of MountainView Vineyard Church.

Today we provide before-school, after-school and holiday care built around safety, fun, connection and growth. Those words shape what staff actually do: supervise well, plan things worth joining, know the children and communicate with parents.

Meet the team

The people who make Kidz.com happen.

History has given us some truly great teams: the Avengers, the 1995–96 Chicago Bulls, the A-Team, the Ghostbusters… and now, the Kidz.com crew.

Meet the people bringing the energy, ideas and heart to every Kidz.com afternoon.
