What happens here?

There is room for different kinds of afternoons.

Some children arrive ready to run. Some want to make something. Some want to find their friends. Some need half an hour to do very little.

A good afternoon has room for all of them. We plan creative, active and social options without organising every minute. Children can join in, try something different or follow an idea of their own.

What matters to us

Good care is about more than having a busy programme.

01 · SAFE

Safety comes first.

Staff are expected to supervise actively, notice what is happening and respond early. Children’s safety and wellbeing come before the activity.

02 · FUN

Fun with some thought behind it.

We plan activities worth joining—not just things to fill time. There is also room for children’s own ideas and free play.

03 · CONNECT

Children should be known.

We want to know names, interests, friendships and when something does not seem quite right. Belonging starts with being noticed.

04 · GROW

Parents shouldn’t have to guess.

We like telling parents the good things we noticed. If something has not gone well, we will talk clearly and work out the next step together.
