How to enrol

Start by emailing Ann.

There is no need to create your own Discover account before contacting us.

Tell Ann what care you are looking for. She will check availability and send the enrolment information and next steps.

  1. Email Ann
    Include your child’s name and school.
  2. Tell us what care you need
    Before school, after school or particular holiday dates.
  3. Add the practical details
    The days required, preferred start date and your phone number.
  4. Wait for confirmation
    Ann will reply with availability and the appropriate enrolment information.
Email Ann

Please don’t include detailed medical or private information in your first email. We will collect that through the appropriate enrolment process.

OSCAR subsidy

Childcare may cost less than you expect.

Kidz.com is an OSCAR-approved programme. Eligible families may receive up to $6.72 per hour, per child, toward before-school, after-school and holiday-programme costs.

The income limits are broader than many families realise. Our subsidy guide explains the current thresholds, who may qualify, how to apply and which section Ann can complete for you.

Tell us what will help

Useful information makes good care easier.

If your child has allergies, medication, additional support needs, worries about new places or anything else we should know, talk with us before their first day.

The goal is not to label children. It is to help our team plan well and support them safely.

Common questions

Quick answers.

What should my child bring?

The final answer will cover water bottles, food, suitable clothing, sun protection and anything required for a particular holiday day.

What happens if my child will be absent?

Please tell us as early as possible, particularly if we are expecting to collect your child from school. Add the preferred absence contact method before launch.

Can somebody else collect my child?

Only authorised collectors can collect a child. Please contact us before collection arrangements change.

How do you use photographs?

We follow the photograph permissions recorded for each child. We will not knowingly use an identifiable photograph outside those permissions.

What if I have a concern?

Please talk with us. We will listen, clarify what has happened and explain what we can do next. If we need time to look into something properly, we will tell you when you can expect to hear back.
