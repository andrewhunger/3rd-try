July holiday programme

Rockets, oceans and a lot of hot glue

The July holidays moved from outer space to the ocean, with plenty of making, running, testing and rebuilding in between.

Draft layout · Replace or approve before launch

Space Week began with bottle rockets outside and quickly spread across the programme. Children designed aliens, made chalk planets on black paper and discovered that space slime is considerably easier to make than it is to clean up.

Things were made to be tested.

The rockets needed launching. The basketball challenges needed challengers. The hot-glue aliens needed judging. Children had opportunities to try an idea, see what happened and change it when the first attempt did not quite work.

Then we headed for the ocean.

Moana Week brought ocean bottles, Te Kā faces, Kakamora bowling and more than one attempt to keep an egg balanced through a relay. Some children wanted the competition. Others stayed with the craft table and worked on details for much longer than expected.

That mix is important to us. A programme can have a strong theme without expecting every child to enjoy it in the same way.
